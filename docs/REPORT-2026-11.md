### What it says

- **Every number reproduces.** The desk can trust a rerun, and the desk now fails a strategy
  back automatically when an official run doesn't reproduce.
- **No trial survives.** The intraday strategies (1–6) lose in almost every year and at every
  cost level. Their stable parameters only mean they lose consistently, not that they're close.
- **Momentum (trial 7) is the only one worth another look.** It stays positive at double and triple
  costs and in 7 of 9 years. It fails because one month's average is too noisy: 11% of resamples
  are below zero, and its Sharpe isn't distinguishable from the best of 12 tries by luck.
- **Parameter sensitivity covers trials 1–6 only.** Trials 7–9 and 11 have their parameters
  compiled in. That doesn't change any verdict, since each already fails other bars.
